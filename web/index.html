Recording checklist
  • Hold the camera still. Use a tripod or rest the phone on something. Any camera movement looks like flow.
  • Point straight at the flow, perpendicular to the surface or light sheet, so distances are the same everywhere in the picture.
  • Add tracer particles that follow the water: ground pepper, coffee grounds, glitter, or chalk dust. A dense, even speckle is ideal, with about 10 or more particles in every patch the size of your thumbnail on screen.
  • Light it brightly and evenly. Avoid reflections and glare spots; side lighting often works best.
  • Use the highest frame rate your phone offers (slow-motion mode is great). Fast frames mean small, measurable movements.
  • Particles should move a few pixels between the two frames, not more than a quarter of the analysis window. Pick frames closer together for fast flow, further apart for slow flow.
  • Put a ruler in the picture, at the same distance as the flow, so you can work out millimetres per pixel.
  • Avoid motion blur: bright light lets the camera use short exposures. Particles should look like sharp dots, not streaks.
  • Phone format: if the video will not open, record in "Most Compatible" (H.264) instead of HEVC/H.265.
